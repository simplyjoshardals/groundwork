# Kestrel & Vane Ltd: Employee FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about the Kestrel & Vane Ltd Employee Handbook (version 3.2). If a question is not answered here, email the People team at people@kestrelandvane.example. Kestrel & Vane Ltd is a fictional company, and this FAQ is a synthetic sample document written for software testing.

## Leave

### Q1. How many days of annual leave do I get?

Full-time employees get 26 days of paid annual leave in each leave year, and the leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. You receive 2 extra days after 3 completed years of service and 4 extra days in total after 6 completed years, so a full-time employee with 6 completed years gets 30 days. Public holidays are in addition to this.

### Q2. Can I carry over unused leave?

Yes. Up to 5 days of unused leave can be carried over into the next leave year. You must use the carried-over days by 30 June, otherwise they are lost.

### Q3. How do I request leave?

Request leave in the People Hub. A manager normally answers within 4 working days. A request covering 10 or more consecutive working days needs at least 6 weeks' notice.

## Sickness

### Q4. What should I do if I am too ill to work?

Tell your line manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence. You need a medical certificate once an absence lasts more than 3 consecutive working days, and you send it to the People team. You get 12 days at full pay each leave year.

## Working pattern

### Q5. How many days a week do I have to be in the office?

At least 2 days a week, and the company's anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday. During the probation period it is 4 days a week.

### Q6. Can I work from another country?

Yes, for up to 20 working days per calendar year, but you need written approval from the Head of People. Send your request at least 5 weeks before you travel.

### Q7. When do I get the home-office stipend?

New employees get a one-off stipend of $450. It is paid with the first payroll after your probation period ends, and the probation period lasts 6 months.

## Expenses and travel

### Q8. How much can I claim for a business trip to Berlin?

Berlin is a Tier 2 city, so you can claim $60 per day for meals and up to $190 per night for a hotel.

### Q9. How soon must I submit an expense claim?

Submit it in the Tallybook portal within 30 days of the expense. The Finance Office rejects older claims unless it agreed an exception in advance.

## IT security

### Q10. What do I do if I lose my laptop?

Report it to the Security Desk within 1 hour of noticing the loss. Email security@kestrelandvane.example or call extension 4417.

## Speaking up, pay and benefits

### Q11. How do I raise a concern about misconduct?

Use the Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider, or email speakup@kestrelandvane.example. Every report is acknowledged within 2 working days, and investigations are normally completed within 20 working days of the investigator being assigned.

### Q12. When am I paid, and what benefits do I get?

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month, or on the Friday before when the 25th falls on a weekend. The company contributes 6% of base salary to your pension if you contribute at least 4%, and 7% in total if you contribute 8% or more. Each employee also has a learning budget of $1,200 per leave year.
